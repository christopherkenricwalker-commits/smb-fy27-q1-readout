Executive summary The story in plain terms

Advanced is carrying a 121 ITF quarter and attach proves it compounds. Three things stand between us and the $500M opportunity: payroll, Advanced pace, and Payments conversion.

We are moving the whole org from selling add-ons to selling QuickBooks as one platform. Reps start with the customer's problem, then show how payroll, payments and the books work together. Advanced is where that conversation starts — and customers are adding to it. Customers with 2+ Advanced features retain at 90%; payroll or accountant-attached customers cancel at 0.4–0.7× the peer rate.

Platform Activation Onsite · Sept 29 – Oct 1

We put sellers, leaders and partners in one room at Mountain View and skipped the slides. A day and a half of live objection handling, platform pitches, and a pitch-off with finalists on the last day.

The human moments are the ones that stick. One seller visited HQ for the first time in 20 years. Another met a colleague of 10+ years in person for the first time. Several took their first work trip ever.

The numbers followed. The week after: EMM's best week of Q1 — $1.93M vs $1.34M forecast, 144 ITF, with IB, OB and AE all over aspiration together for the first time this quarter. Thank you to Whitney Sieck, Milka Akinloye-Grimm, and Kim Amundson.
