In the last week of September we put sellers, leaders and partners in one room in Mountain View. We skipped the slides. A day and a half of live objection handling, platform pitches, and a pitch-off with finalists on the last day.

The people stories are the ones that stuck. One seller visited HQ for the first time in 20 years. Another met a colleague of 10+ years in person for the first time. Several took their first work trip ever.

The numbers followed. The week after: EMM's best week of Q1 — $1.93M against a $1.34M forecast, 144 ITF, with Inbound, Outbound and AE all over aspiration together for the first time this quarter.

Thank you to Whitney Sieck, who ran the event soup to nuts, and to Milka Akinloye-Grimm and Kim Amundson.
