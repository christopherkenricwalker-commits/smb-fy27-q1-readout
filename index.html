EMM's best week of Q1, the week after

In the last week of September we put sellers, leaders and partners in one room in Mountain View for SMB and EMM Platform Activation, our Built for More onsite. We skipped the slides. We spent a day and a half practicing: live objection handling, platform pitches, and a pitch-off with finalists on the last day.

The people stories are the ones that stuck. One seller visited HQ for the first time in 20 years. Another met a colleague of more than ten years in person for the first time. Several took their first work trip ever.

The numbers followed. The week after we got back, EMM posted its best week of Q1: $1.93M against a $1.34M forecast, 144 ITF, with Inbound, Outbound and AE all over aspiration together for the first time this quarter.

Thank you to Whitney Sieck, who ran the event soup to nuts, and to Milka Akinloye-Grimm and Kim Amundson for the big and little things.
