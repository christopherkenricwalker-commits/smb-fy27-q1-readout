Executive summary The story in plain terms

Advanced is carrying a 121 ITF quarter and attach proves it compounds. Three things stand between us and the $500M: payroll, where Sales holds 74% of the miss; Advanced pace, which has eased; and Payments conversion.

We are moving the whole org from selling add-ons to selling QuickBooks as one platform. Reps start with the customer's problem, then show how payroll, payments and the books work together. Advanced is where that conversation starts — and customers are adding to it. Next, we are bringing the same conversation to Workforce and Payments outside Advanced.
